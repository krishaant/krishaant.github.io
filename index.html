Some of my projects

Air Quality & Income Inequality: Canadian Metropolitan Areas

Found that worse air quality is linked to higher income inequality across 63 Canadian metro areas (adjusted R² = 0.78), using spline interpolation on air-quality data and OLS regressions in R, validated with RESET, VIF, and White's tests. With 3 co-authors (Zane Williams, Aiden Cathcart, and Jiaqi Lei); presented at LSE's Knowledge Beyond Boundaries conference (Jun 2025).

R OLS Regression Environmental Econ

Does Student Sentiment Move Professor Pay?

Modeled whether professor salaries relate to student satisfaction by fitting OLS regressions on BERT-based sentiment analysis of RateMyProfessors reviews, scraping 10,000+ records across three datasets.

Python NLP OLS Regression

Does the Income Gap Vary by Immigrant Generation?

Ran a sequence of regressions of income on first- vs. second-generation immigrant status using the 2021 Canadian Census PUMF, layering in controls (gender, age, education, labour force status, province) and an interaction model to test whether the gap itself depends on education and labour force participation.

R Causal Inference Census Data

R Clustering Marketing Analytics
